Entities: named and numeric references

Basic named entities

&amp; = & | &lt; = < | &gt; = > | &quot; = " | &nbsp; = [ ] (non-breaking space)

Latin-1 named

© ® ™ ° ± ² ³ µ ¶ § · « » ¼ ½ ¾ ¡ ¿ ß æ ç é è ê ë à â ä ö ü ñ å ø

Currency and punctuation

£ ¥ ¢ ¤ € (euro may fall back) – (ndash) — (mdash) … (hellip) ‘single’ “double”

Symbols

← ↑ → ↓ (arrows) × ÷ ≠ ≤ ≥ ± (maths) • (bullet) † ‡ ‰

Greek

α β γ δ π σ ω Α Δ Π Σ Ω

Numeric references

A = A (decimal 65) | A = A (hex 41) | © = copyright | ← = left arrow | π = pi

Non-breaking behaviour

word word word word word word word - these are all joined by &nbsp; and must wrap as one unit.

Unmappable characters degrade to a substitute: ☃ (snowman) あ (hiragana a)
